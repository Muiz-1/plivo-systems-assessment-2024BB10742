2. Key Architectural Decisions

  • Precise Packet Budgeting (19/20 Duplication Ratio): Transmitting duplicates for 19 out of 20 frames yields exactly 2,925 packets (479,700 bytes), utilizing 99.9% of the allowed 2.00x bandwidth budget (2,926 max packets).
  • Immediate Duplication over Staggered Duplication: Sending duplicates immediately gives each frame the maximum available time window to traverse the lossy network. Holding duplicates for temporal separation added 20 ms of latency, causing deadline miss spikes.
  • O(1) Sequence De-duplication: Fast bitset tracking in receiver.c filters duplicate arrivals instantly without dynamic memory allocations or lookup overhead.
  • Socket Ring Buffer Tuning: Configured 1 MB OS receive/send buffers (SO_RCVBUF / SO_SNDBUF) and low-latency TOS flags to prevent kernel-level packet drops.
